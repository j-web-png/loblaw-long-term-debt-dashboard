Key findings

  • Q1 reported debt was approximately 23.71% lower year over year.
  • Q2 reported debt was approximately 27% lower year over year.
  • PC Financial held-for-sale classification affects comparability.
  • Lower reported debt does not necessarily represent cash debt repayment.

Recommendations

  • Prioritize sustainable repayment of higher-interest debt.
  • Monitor debt-to-EBITDA and liquidity.
  • Maintain strong operating cash flow.
  • Separate debt repayments from accounting reclassifications.

Limitations

  • Only four observations are included.
  • Accounting reclassifications affect year-over-year comparability.
  • The charts do not establish causation.
  • Quarter-end balances do not show all borrowing and repayment activity.
